## Robot

All lengths were taken with the second-row seats slid as far forward as they go and the front seats moved forward far enough to allow that. Where a start point is not given below, the submitter did not state it.

- 75 in (6 ft 3 in) to the back of the second-row seats.
- 95 in (7 ft 11 in) to the cup rest. Which cup rest was not stated.
- 100 in (8 ft 4 in) from the back of the driver's headrest to the bottom of the liftgate opening. Straight line or along the floor was not stated.
- Liftgate opening: 4 ft (48 in) wide, almost 58 in on the diagonal. Where across the opening the width was taken was not stated.

Conflict, recorded as given: 95 in to the cup rest is less than 100 in to the driver's headrest, yet the front console cup holders sit ahead of the headrest. Either the cup rest is a second-row one or the two numbers have different endpoints.

Second-row seats not removable: partly confirmed. Toyota's [2022 Sienna Quick Reference Guide](https://assets.sia.toyota.com/publications/en/om-s/OM22C2QRG/pdf/OM22C2QRG.pdf) (p. 25) gives a removal procedure only for the second-row center seat "(if equipped)", the middle seat of 8-passenger models; the outboard seats get only slide, tip-up and fold procedures. Toyota does not say outright that the outboard seats cannot be removed.

Trim matters: confirmed. Toyota's [2021 Sienna launch release](https://pressroom.toyota.com/?generate_pdf=60569) says LE and some XLE are 8-seat with a long-slide second row and a stowable middle seat, while some XLE, XSE, Limited and Platinum are 7-seat with Super Long Slide captain's chairs that slide 25 in (Limited and Platinum FWD add ottomans). The 75 in figure therefore applies to the Super Long Slide seats, not the 8-seat configuration.

Measured by the submitter, 2026-09-29, second row fully forward. Seat-removal and trim claims checked against Toyota's 2022 Quick Reference Guide and 2021 launch release, 2026-09-29. Cup-rest location, headrest measurement path and liftgate width position not stated; whether the outboard seats can be removed at all unverified.
